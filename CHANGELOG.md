# Changelog

and this project adheres to [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html).

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- Cross replica signals in `Orvano.SharedKernel/Signals/`: `SignalKind` (`job`, `cache`, `session`), `ISignalBus`, `ICacheInvalidator`, `ISignalHandler`, `ISignalConnectionObserver`, and `ISignalConnectionState`, implemented in `Orvano.Infrastructure`. A signal enlisted on a platform `DbContext` is sent with `pg_notify` on the `orvano_signal` channel on that transaction's connection just before it commits: one notification per kind, nothing on a rollback, and only the committing attempt's values under the execution strategy. `OrvanoTransactions.InTransactionAsync` runs work in a fresh transaction per attempt. Enlisting with no open transaction, on a context that is not a platform database context, or on one that joined its transaction with `UseTransaction` throws `InvalidOperationException`. Payloads are `{"v":1,"k":"<kind>","d":[…]}`, at most 999 bytes, collapsed to `["*"]` when larger; a malformed payload is logged and ignored. (spec 0013 AC-1 to AC-5; `faf3ad5` (PR 42), `4076b5d` (PR 43), `6c92b1b` (PR 51))
- Cache invalidation across replicas. `HybridCache` is now signal aware with a 60 second default expiry: `ICacheInvalidator.Invalidate` removes the tags on the writer's own instance after the commit, before `CommitAsync` returns, and on every other instance within 5 seconds. Every `serve` process, in any role, runs one listener with a heartbeat. The instance starts disconnected, and while disconnected (a dropped connection, or no heartbeat for `HeartbeatTimeout`) cache reads skip the cache, observers hear `OnDisconnectedAsync` and get a fallback poll, and reconnects back off with full jitter; each connect clears the whole cache before reads use it again. Handlers run on a dispatch loop of their own, each call cut off after `HandlerTimeout`. (spec 0013 AC-6 to AC-10; `4076b5d` (PR 43), `69df8b5` (PR 48), `6c92b1b` (PR 51))
- `ORVANO_SIGNALS__HEARTBEATINTERVAL` (default 30 s), `__HEARTBEATTIMEOUT` (90 s), `__RECONNECTBACKOFFCAP` (30 s), `__FALLBACKPOLLINTERVAL` (5 s), and `__HANDLERTIMEOUT` (10 s). Each must be at least 1 second, the timeout at least twice the interval, and the handler timeout at most the heartbeat timeout, or startup stops naming the setting. The listener logs connects and disconnects and reports the metrics `orvano.infrastructure.signals.connected`, `.sent` (confirmed commits only), and `.received`. (spec 0013 AC-11, AC-13; `4076b5d` (PR 43), `6c92b1b` (PR 51))
- The job queue in `Orvano.SharedKernel/Jobs/`: `IJob`, `IJobHandler<TJob>`, `IJobQueue`, `JobContext`, `EnqueueOptions`, `JobKindOptions`, and `AddJobHandler<TJob, THandler>`. `orvano migrate` creates `jobs.job` and `jobs.schedule`, first in `MigrationOrder`. `EnqueueAsync` inserts the job in the caller's open platform transaction (or in its own) and sends one `job` signal on commit; an optional dedupe key is unique per kind and project. Only `serve` with the `worker` role runs jobs: it claims due jobs with `FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED`, renews each lease every third of it, and fences each outcome by attempt. A failed, timed out, or unreadable job retries after a jittered delay up to min(1 hour, 10 s × 2^(attempt − 1)) and ends `dead` with its last error. A job with a project runs as `Caller.System` in that project's scope. Workers wake on the `job` signal, on each signal connect, and on the poll. `CancelProjectJobsAsync` marks a project's jobs `dead`. (spec 0013 AC-14 to AC-23, AC-30; `0e27e1d` (PR 53), `c17cff3` (PR 54), `e6bbe39` (PR 58))
- Recurring jobs with `AddJobSchedule<TJob>`, a five field cron expression parsed by `Cronos`, fired once per occurrence however many workers run. The built in `jobs.cleanup` schedule (daily at 03:17 UTC) deletes succeeded jobs after 7 days and dead jobs after 30. A bad kind or schedule registration stops startup with a message naming it and exit code 1. (spec 0013 AC-24, AC-25; `c17cff3` (PR 54), `51015f2` (PR 56))
- `ORVANO_JOBS__POLLINTERVAL` (default 5 s, 1 s to 5 minutes) and `ORVANO_JOBS__CONCURRENCY` (default the smallest of processor count × 2, 16, and the pool limit; explicit values 1 to 256). The runner reports `orvano.infrastructure.jobs.enqueued`, `.attempts`, `.duration`, `.lag`, `.running`, and `.schedules.fired`, tagged with no ids, and payloads and dedupe keys never appear in logs above Debug. (spec 0013 AC-26, AC-27; `c17cff3` (PR 54))
- The module outbox in `Orvano.SharedKernel/Outbox/`. A module registers versioned event types with `AddOutbox`, maps `<schema>.outbox` with `HasOutbox()`, and publishes with `IOutbox.Publish` in the caller's `SaveChanges` and transaction; a before commit check refuses an event that was never saved. On the `worker` role the dispatcher turns each committed event into one job per subscriber registered with `AddEventHandler<TEvent, THandler>`, so each subscriber retries on its own in the event's project scope. Batches hold 100 events, halved after a failed or cut batch (DSP-5). The built in `outbox.cleanup` schedule (daily at 03:37 UTC) deletes dispatched events after 7 days. The metrics are `orvano.infrastructure.outbox.published`, `.dispatched`, and `.lag`. No module publishes yet. (spec 0013 AC-31 to AC-41; `4fef719` (PR 60), `364f715` (PR 61))
- Module isolation checks for the background runtime: modules cannot remove cache entries except through `ICacheInvalidator`, use `TransactionSignals`, `SignalPayload`, `JobAccessScope`, or `OutboxEvent`, wait on or open Npgsql connections, or name `pg_notify`, `orvano_signal`, the jobs tables, or an outbox table in their source. An architecture test allows `AccessContext.Establish` only from the access layer and `JobAccessScope`. (spec 0013 AC-12, AC-28, AC-42; `4076b5d` (PR 43), `6c92b1b` (PR 51), `c17cff3` (PR 54), `364f715` (PR 61), `5b0f633` (PR 64))
- `Orvano.Testing` gains `JobsDriver` (`RunDueAsync` dispatches pending events and runs every due job on the current clock, `DispatchAsync` runs one dispatch), `ManualClock`, and two server hosts on one database. Every time the queue, runner, and outbox use comes from the injected `TimeProvider`. (spec 0013 AC-29, AC-43; `c17cff3` (PR 54), `364f715` (PR 61))
- The caller resolver chain and endpoint default deny. A credential kind implements `ICallerResolver` and registers with `AddCallerResolver<T>` at a fixed order in `CallerResolverOrder`; the access layer runs the chain, establishes the caller and project, and answers `401 unauthorized` when no caller resolves, a resolver fails (the reason is logged, never sent), or a key's project differs from the route's. Every endpoint must declare exactly one kind of requirement or `AllowPublicAccess`: `serve` refuses to start otherwise, printing each wrong endpoint and exiting 1 with no stack trace, and an undeclared endpoint added later answers `404`. The health checks and console page are public. (spec 0012 RSV-1 to RSV-5, EPT-1 to EPT-5, AC-9, AC-13; `f44dd15` (PR 36), `77f84b7` (PR 37), `7e2163a` (PR 38), `5f74df2` (PR 41))
- The test that an end user label change reaches every server instance, run on two hosts through the signal aware cache. This is the access control AC-10 the entry above waited on. (spec 0012 AC-10, spec 0013 AC-7; `e418202` (PR 44))
- The access control core in `Orvano.SharedKernel/Access/`, the one permissions model databases, storage, realtime, and functions will share. A grant is the text token `<action>:<role>`: `read`, `create`, `update`, `delete`, or `execute` for `any`, `guests`, `users`, `user:<uuid>`, or `label:<name>`. `Grant` and `Role` parse it strictly and refuse the reserved `team:`, `member:`, and `verified` forms. `GrantSet.Normalize` turns input into the stored array: `write:<role>` expanded, each action checked against its target (`create` only on tables and buckets, `execute` only on functions), guests and end users limited to their own roles (tokens naming other roles survive their update), deduplicated, sorted, and at most 100. `GrantSet.DefaultsFor` gives an end user `read`, `update`, and `delete` on the rows and files they create; everyone else starts with none. (spec 0012 AC-1, AC-6, AC-7; `b59c56e` (PR 32))
- `Caller`, the one kind every request resolves to (guest, end user, secret key, run key, operator, system), with its role set, and `AccessEvaluator`, the one answer to "may this caller do this?". It allows, filters rows or files by the caller's match tokens when row security is on, answers `404 not_found` for a resource the caller cannot see, and `403 forbidden` for a visible one. Secret keys, run keys, operators, and the system skip grants. `WhereGranted` in `Orvano.SharedKernel/Data/` runs the row filter in the query as a PostgreSQL array overlap (`&&`) with the tokens as one parameter, and a test against a real database proves it returns exactly the allowed rows with correct `limit`, `offset`, `count`, and bulk write counts. End user labels come through `ICallerRolesSource`, which feature 14 implements; the test that a label change reaches every server instance (AC-10) waits for feature 61. (spec 0012 AC-2 to AC-5; `b59c56e` (PR 32))
- The API key scope catalog with a ceiling per key type (`Scopes.Validate` refuses `users.impersonate` and `client.auth.signup` without `client.auth`), the console permission list with `owner` holding all of it, and the endpoint methods `RequireScope`, `RequireClientApi`, and `RequireConsolePermission`. They add endpoint metadata that `UseAccessRequirements()`, placed right after `UseRouting`, enforces: a key without the scope gets `403 insufficient_scope`, a publishable key with that client API off gets `403 forbidden`, and an operator who is not a member, or names a project outside the organization, gets `404 not_found` (a member without the permission gets `403 forbidden`). An endpoint that declares a requirement refuses to run if the middleware is missing. (spec 0012 AC-8, AC-11; `b59c56e` (PR 32), `a38e910` (PR 33))
- `ICallerContext` and `IProjectContext`, registered by `AddAccessContext()` and set only by the access layer (the project only after its check passes), plus the `Error`, `Result<T>`, and `error.ToProblem()` vocabulary for expected failures. `ToProblem` adds `code`, `traceId`, field errors, and the error's extensions. No credential resolver sets the caller yet; features 13 and 14 add them. (spec 0012 PRJ-3, spec 0002 ERR-1 to ERR-4; `b59c56e` (PR 32), `a38e910` (PR 33))
- Architecture tests that fail the build when the access core references an Npgsql, EF Core, or SQL type, when `WhereGranted` leaves the data namespace, when anything outside the access layer implements the caller or project context, when `IProjectContext` gains a public setter, or when `Orvano.SharedKernel` shows its internals to anything but test projects. (spec 0012 AC-9, AC-12; `b59c56e` (PR 32))
- The shared data conventions every platform `DbContext` builds on, in `Orvano.SharedKernel/Data/`. `UseOrvanoConventions(schema, ...)` sets the module's schema, snake case names, the `<schema>.__ef_migrations_history` table, and the default retry, and registers a scoped `TimestampInterceptor` that stamps `CreatedAt` and `UpdatedAt` from the injected `TimeProvider` (and stamps the owner when an owned value changes). `Entity` gives every row an app made uuid v7 `Id`. `HasTextEnum` stores an enum as snake case text guarded by a named CHECK, `HasAnchor` is the only way to point into another module's schema (organization, project, operator, end user, each with its allowed delete rules), `[DataClass]` marks each stored text or bytes property as secret, personal, or public, and `EncryptedValue` holds ciphertext that never prints or serializes its bytes. `EmailAddress.Normalize` and the `ISecretProtector` interface come with it. No production table exists yet; features 13 onward create them from the spec's catalog. (spec 0011 AC-1 to AC-5, AC-9, AC-11; `f100b4c` (PR 30))
- Architecture tests that walk every server `DbContext` model and fail the build on a context that skips `UseOrvanoConventions` or maps a table outside its schema, a cross schema FK to anything but the four anchors (or with a delete rule REF-3 forbids), a text, bytes, address, JSON, or `EncryptedValue` property without `[DataClass]`, a store generated key, two contexts sharing a schema, and a module context missing from `MigrationOrder`. Each rule is proven by a deliberately broken sample context. (spec 0011 AC-3, AC-6 to AC-8; `f100b4c` (PR 30))
- The platform data model catalog: every table later features build, with its owning module, columns, anchors, delete rules, data classes, and retention, plus a traceability table from scope features 12 to 60 to their tables. (spec 0011 CAT-1, CAT-2, AC-10; `f100b4c` (PR 30))
- Community files at the repo root: `SECURITY.md` (private vulnerability reporting, supported versions, response targets, scope, and safe harbor), `CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md` (Contributor Covenant 3.0), `CONTRIBUTING.md` (setup pointers, branches and PRs, the DCO sign off and how to fix a failed check, AI tools, Windows notes), `SUPPORT.md`, and `TRADEMARKS.md`. `README.md` links them from a new Community section. The project security address is still a placeholder, to be filled in before the repo goes public. (spec 0010 VRP, COC, COM; `65f6379` (old PR 4))
- Issue forms for bug reports and feature requests, with blank issues turned off and links to Discussions Q&A and private reporting, plus `.github/CODEOWNERS`. (spec 0010 ISS; `65f6379` (old PR 4))
- A `dco` sign off job inside `ci-gate` that runs on every PR. It lists each commit whose sign off is missing or doesn't match its author, and prints the `git rebase --signoff` fix. Merge commits are skipped. (spec 0010 AC-7, spec 0009 CTB-3; `65f6379` (old PR 4))
- `tooling/scripts/github-settings.mjs`, which keeps the GitHub repo and org settings (the `main` ruleset, security features, labels, Discussions categories) as code. Check mode is read only and exits 1 on drift; `--apply` writes the differences with your own `gh` login. It never runs in CI. (spec 0010 SET, RUL, ORG; `65f6379` (old PR 4))
- `tooling/scripts/export-template.mjs`, which writes a template repo's tree from `tooling/starters/**` into a clone, with `--check` to report drift. The starters gain CodeQL and dependency review workflows (the Dart export has no CodeQL) and a new `tooling/starters/org/` source for the org `.github` repo. The template repos and the org `.github` repo themselves don't exist yet. (spec 0010 TPL; `65f6379` (old PR 4))
- `node --test` suites for the dco job and both scripts, run by `pnpm test:tooling`. (spec 0010 AC-19; `65f6379` (old PR 4), `8204a94` (old PR 5))

### Changed

- `ORVANO_DATABASE__POOLSIZE` below 6 now fails startup (it took 1 to 1000 before; the default stays 30). Job concurrency may be at most `ORVANO_DATABASE__POOLSIZE` / 2 − 2, the other connections kept for requests and for the job runner's and outbox dispatcher's loops; an explicit `ORVANO_JOBS__CONCURRENCY` above that stops startup naming both settings. (spec 0013 AC-40, OPL-1, spec 0007 JOB-4; `c17cff3` (PR 54), `e6bbe39` (PR 58), `364f715` (PR 61))
- `serve` now gives running jobs 25 seconds to stop and be released on shutdown, and the reference compose sets `stop_grace_period: 35s` on the `orvano` service so Docker waits for it. (spec 0013 OUT-6; `c17cff3` (PR 54))
- The server now depends on `Cronos` 0.13.0 (MIT) for cron schedules and `Microsoft.Extensions.Caching.Hybrid` 10.11.0 (MIT) for the cache. (spec 0013; `4076b5d` (PR 43), `c17cff3` (PR 54))
- The health checks and console routes use `AllowPublicAccess` instead of `AllowAnonymous`, and the health checks answer `GET` only; another method answers `404`. (spec 0012 EPT-5; `77f84b7` (PR 37), `5f74df2` (PR 41))
- `RequireConsolePermission` on a route with neither `{organizationId}` nor `{projectId}` now fails startup instead of the first request. `AccessEvaluator.MayChangeItemGrants` takes the row security flag, and `Caller` is a class rather than a record, so two callers are equal only by reference. (spec 0012 EVL-2, CPL-3; `5001705` (PR 35))
- Specs 0002, 0006, 0007, and 0011 now point to spec 0012. The catalog gains `databases.table` and the grant columns on buckets, files, and functions, `api_key.scopes` takes spec 0012's scope lists with a CHECK per key type, and `audit_event.actor_kind` gains `function`. End user labels and resource grants count as security related cache entries (60 second cap), and the feature 9 follow ups in specs 0006 and 0011 are closed. (spec 0012 AC-8, AC-9; `b59c56e` (PR 32))
- `Orvano.SharedKernel` now references `EFCore.NamingConventions` and the Npgsql EF Core provider, and spec 0002 LAY-3 allows the naming package. `PostgresFixture` gains `ConnectionString(database)` and `CreateDatabaseAsync(prefix)`, so a test class can get its own database on the one shared container. (spec 0011 build plan step 1, spec 0002 TST-5; `f100b4c` (PR 30))
- CodeQL now uploads its results on every event, fork and Dependabot PRs included, so the code scanning gate can be added once both uploads are proven. (spec 0010 RUL-5; `65f6379` (old PR 4))
- The `dco` job skips a commit authored by a bot only when a bot also opened the PR. In a PR opened by a person, a bot authored commit is checked like any other and fails without a matching sign off, with the `git commit --amend --reset-author -s` fix. If the PR author type is missing, it counts as a person. This closes a gap where anyone could claim the skip through a git author email. (spec 0010 DCO-2; `19f6a6d` (old PR 6), `eda5a0a` (old PR 7))

### Fixed

- An outbox delivery larger than the 65,536 byte job payload limit now fails its dispatch batch, which then halves. Every dispatch batch that marks events sends the `job` signal on commit, so a batch cut short by another dispatcher is followed by another pass. After commit actions no longer run when a before commit check or `pg_notify` fails before the `COMMIT` statement. (spec 0013 DSP-5, DSP-7, SND-1, SND-3; `5b0f633` (PR 64))
- The job runner frees a job's slot whenever its attempt ends, counts a fault around the handler or in the payload reader as a failed attempt, bounds each claim by 10 seconds so a stalled claim no longer holds up shutdown, and runs lease loss and timeout cancels off the timer thread. A schedule whose job kind needs a project now fails startup. (spec 0013 SCH-6, OUT-6, OUT-7; `51015f2` (PR 56))
- A signal handler or connection observer that hangs no longer stalls the listener: each call is bounded, and a connection that went silent is closed in the background, so the disconnect, fallback polls, and reconnect are not delayed. (spec 0013 LSN-6; `69df8b5` (PR 48))
- On a connection that went silent without closing, the heartbeat send now stops at the heartbeat deadline. Before, only Npgsql's 30 second command timeout ended it, so the instance stayed connected past `HeartbeatTimeout` and served cache entries other instances had invalidated. (spec 0013 AC-8, LSN-3; `a9e0590` (PR 46))
- CORS now runs between routing and the access layer, so a browser preflight is answered without a credential. A request that matches no endpoint gets routing's empty `404` from the access layer, so a later middleware branch cannot answer it. Route organization and project ids parse only in canonical uuid form. (spec 0007 COR-1, spec 0012 EPT-1; `5f74df2` (PR 41))
- Grant and scope problems now quote each token short, with control characters made visible, list at most 20 problems, and stop reading a list after 500 entries. A guest or end user writing table, bucket, or function grants gets `403 forbidden` (WRT-6), and a row's own `update` grant lets a client change its grants only while row security is on (EVL-2). A `default` `Result` carries an error, and an error's own extensions can no longer replace `code`, `traceId`, or `errors` in its problem. (spec 0012 GRM-2, SCP-3, WRT-6, EVL-2, spec 0002 ERR-1; `5001705` (PR 35))
- A caller refused by `RequireScope`, `RequireClientApi`, or `RequireConsolePermission` now gets its `403` or `404` before the request body is bound and validated. Before, the checks ran as endpoint filters, so a refused caller who sent a bad body got a `400` that named the fields. (spec 0012 DEN-5; `a38e910` (PR 33))
- A rate limited `Error` now answers its `429` with a `Retry-After` header taken from `retryAfterSeconds`. Before, the header was missing. (spec 0002 ERR-4; `a38e910` (PR 33))
- `export-template.mjs` refuses to write into the core repo (including a symlink, a folder inside or above it, or a worktree of it), and refuses a clone with uncommitted changes to tracked files unless you pass `--force`. Before, a wrong target could delete tracked files. (`8204a94` (old PR 5))
- `export-template.mjs` no longer crashes on a second run before you commit a removal, `--check` no longer calls an already removed file stale, and the `.github` target no longer needs the core's `origin/main`. (`8204a94` (old PR 5))
- `github-settings.mjs` exits 2 on a GraphQL error or an unexpected API response, so exit 1 always means drift. (`8204a94` (old PR 5))
- `CONTRIBUTING.md` (core and org) points newcomers to open issues without the `triage` label. (`8204a94` (old PR 5))
