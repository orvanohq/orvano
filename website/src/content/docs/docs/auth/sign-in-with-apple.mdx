Sign in with Apple works like [Sign in with Google](/docs/auth/sign-in-with-google/): the SDK sends the browser to Apple, and Orvano finishes the exchange. On iOS and macOS, a Flutter app signs in natively instead; see [Native sign in](/docs/auth/native-sign-in/). Apps on the App Store that offer another provider must offer Apple too.

Apple has no client secret to copy: Orvano makes a fresh one from your Sign in with Apple key whenever it needs one.

## 1. Set up Apple

In the Apple Developer site, under **Certificates, Identifiers and Profiles**:

1. **App ID**: on your app's identifier, turn on **Sign in with Apple**. Its bundle ID is what native sign in uses.
2. **Services ID**: create one (for example `com.acme.shop.web`) for sign in on the web, and turn on **Sign in with Apple** for it. Under **Configure**, add your Orvano's domain, and as a **Return URL** the **Callback URL** from the Apple dialog on the **Sign in methods** page. Apple needs a real https domain, not `localhost`.
3. **Key**: create a key with **Sign in with Apple** turned on, and download the `.p8` file. Apple lets you download it once. Note its **Key ID**, and your **Team ID** (top right of the site).

## 2. Fill in the Apple dialog

| Field | What goes in it |
|---|---|
| **Services ID** | The Services ID, for sign in by redirect |
| **Bundle IDs** | Your app's bundle IDs, one per line, for native sign in |
| **Team ID** | Your 10 character Team ID |
| **Key ID** | The key's 10 character ID |
| **Private key (.p8)** | The whole `.p8` file, pasted |

Turn on **Enabled** and select **Save**. The card says **Ready: Redirect** once the Services ID, Team ID, key ID, and key are set, and **Native** once a bundle ID is too.

Apple sends back the user's name only the first time they sign in to your app, and Orvano keeps it as the user's name. Users may hide their email; Apple then gives a private relay address (`…@privaterelay.appleid.com`), which counts as verified and forwards to them.

## When a user leaves

Apple requires apps to revoke a user's Sign in with Apple when they delete their account. Orvano does it for you: deleting the user, unlinking Apple, or claiming the account queues a job that tells Apple, with a client secret made for the client ID the user signed in with. Deletion never waits for Apple, and the job retries while Apple is down.

## Limits

The same per IP limits apply as for Google; see [Limits](/docs/auth/sign-in-with-google/#limits).
