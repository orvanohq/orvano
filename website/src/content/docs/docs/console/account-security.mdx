Your console account holds the keys to every project you can reach, so protect it with a second step at sign in. Open the account menu and select **Security**. The page has two parts: **Authenticator app** and **Passkeys**.

## Turn on an authenticator app

1. Under **Authenticator app**, select **Turn on**.
2. Scan the QR code with an app such as 1Password, Google Authenticator, or Microsoft Authenticator. If you can't scan it, type the key shown beside it into the app (**Copy key** copies it).
3. Enter the 6 digit code the app shows and select **Verify**.
4. Save the 10 recovery codes that appear: **Copy codes** or **Download**, then **I saved them**. They show only this once. Each one signs you in once if you lose your phone.

Turning it on signs you out everywhere else. From now on, signing in with your password asks for a code: see [Sign in with a second step](#sign-in-with-a-second-step).

Once it's on, the section shows when you turned it on and how many recovery codes are left. **Make new codes** replaces them with 10 new ones (the old ones stop working), and **Turn off** removes the app and the codes.

## Add a passkey

A passkey signs you in with your face, fingerprint, or device PIN, with no password, and it counts as both steps. Under **Passkeys**, select **Add a passkey** and follow your browser's prompt. Each passkey is listed with when it was added and last used, and whether it is **Synced** (kept by a password manager or your phone's keychain) or **Device bound** (a security key). **Rename** gives it a name you'll recognize, and **Remove** deletes it.

Console passkeys need Orvano on `https` with a domain name, or on `localhost`. An install reached by an IP address or by plain `http` on another host has no console passkeys; use an authenticator app there.

## Confirm it's you

Changes to your security (turning the app on or off, new recovery codes, adding or removing a passkey) need a recent check. When yours is older than 10 minutes, a **Confirm it's you** dialog asks for a code from your app, a recovery code, or a passkey, and then the change goes through. Without an authenticator app, the dialog offers your passkey, or **Sign in again**, which brings you back to this page afterwards.

## Sign in with a second step

On the **Sign in** page, an account with an authenticator app first enters its email and password, then sees **Two step verification**: enter the code from your app, or select **Use a passkey** or **Use a recovery code**. The step lasts 5 minutes; after that, or after 5 wrong codes, you start over.

**Sign in with a passkey** skips both steps, and so does picking a passkey from the email field's suggestions.

Each change to your security emails you an alert through the [install's email server](/docs/self-hosting/email-delivery/), when it has one.

## Locked out

Lost your phone and your recovery codes? Another way in is a passkey. Without one, someone with access to the server can reset your second step with [`orvano mfa reset`](/docs/self-hosting/upgrade/#recover-a-locked-out-console-account).
