## Why it happens

The email and password don't match a user in the project, or the password given to confirm a change (like a new email) is wrong. The message is the same either way, so nobody can learn which emails have accounts.

## How to fix

Show a message like "Wrong email or password" and let the person try again, or offer a [password reset](/docs/auth/password-reset/). Don't tell them whether the email exists.
