## Why it happens

The email and password don't match a user in the project, or the password given to confirm a change is missing or wrong. Changing the email, deleting the account, adding an authenticator app or a passkey, and linking a provider all ask a user who has a password for it, even right after signing in. The message is the same either way, so nobody can learn which emails have accounts.

## How to fix

At sign in, show a message like "Wrong email or password" and let the person try again, or offer a [password reset](/docs/auth/password-reset/). Don't tell them whether the email exists. For a change, ask for the current password (check `user.hasPassword`) and send it with the call.
