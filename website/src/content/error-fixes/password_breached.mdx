## Why it happens

The project turned on the breached password check, and Have I Been Pwned lists the new password in a known data breach. Orvano sends the service only the first 5 characters of the password's SHA-1 hash, never the password, and compares the answer on your server. Passwords people already have keep working; the check applies only at sign up, on a password change, on a reset, and when a server creates a user. If the service is slow or down, the password is accepted.

## How to fix

Ask the person for a different password, and tell them this one has leaked before, so they change it wherever else they use it. To stop the check, turn off **Refuse breached passwords** on the Passwords card of the project's **Security** page in the console.
