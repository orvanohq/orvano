## Why it happens

The provider answered with an error, or its answer failed a check: a refused code, an ID token that doesn't verify, a GitHub App without the "Email addresses: read" permission, or a Microsoft account outside the configured tenant.

## How to fix

Check the provider's settings in the console against its guide: the client ID and secret, the callback URL registered at the provider, GitHub's email permission, and Microsoft's tenant. Then try again.
