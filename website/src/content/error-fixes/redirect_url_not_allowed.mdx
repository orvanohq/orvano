## Why it happens

A redirect URL for an email link is malformed, or its host or scheme isn't one of the project's platforms. Orvano only sends people back to places you registered, so a link can't send them to a stranger's site.

## How to fix

Add a platform for the URL in the console under **Platforms**: a Web platform with its hostname, or your app's scheme for a mobile deep link. Then send the full URL, including the scheme.
